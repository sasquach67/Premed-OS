Frame 2 · the three expiry states, and the proof that nothing else moves
Ruling R6
A credential expiring changes nothing else
You renew it. HQ reminds you, and then gets out of the way.
The reminder escalates. That is all it does.

Three states, one job: tell you before it lapses. There is no blocking, no disabled control, and nothing turns off. An expired credential in HQ is a note on a list.

What actually catches someone who stopped working
Not the credential date. If shifts stop appearing, the stale-exposure alert says so after a few weeks, and it is reading your actual log rather than guessing from a card in your wallet. That is the honest signal, and it works whether or not you ever entered a credential.

🐏
Why a switcher and not one combined chart
Bars plus a cumulative line on a second axis would fit both in one frame, and it is a well-known way to mislead: two scales, and the reader picks whichever crossing looks meaningful. Two clean views with one toggle is more honest and easier to read. The toggle is a filter, so it uses the same solid segmented control as the Assignments view switcher rather than a glass mode pill.

The case for cutting it lean cut
Nobody judges it. Clinical's signals are hours, recency, and continuity (§2.3). Variety of setting is not one of them, and §8 already calls it "minor".
Shadowing owns breadth. Specialty coverage is a real metric there and the app already tracks it properly. Repeating the idea here, weakly, blurs which pillar answers which question.
It restates the site list. Expanding your positions already shows Orange County EMS at 396h and UNC Children's at 138h. This is that, grouped.
It invites bucket-filling. A student who sees "Clinic 18h" reads a gap and goes looking for clinic hours, chasing a balance nobody asked for.
The one argument for keeping it: at application time, "EMS, inpatient, ED and clinic" is a sentence worth writing, and this is where you would notice you can write it. That is a writing-time use, which means it belongs in the export preview, not on the pillar page.
🐏
Where it would go if it survives
Not as a panel on Clinical. As one line inside the AMCAS export preview, next to the scope checklist, at the moment you are describing the role. Same pattern as everything else that turned out to be writing-time rather than tracking-time.

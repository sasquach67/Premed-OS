Front-desk and wayfinding roles are real service, but you have no responsibility for a patient. The test schools use is whether you were close enough to smell the patient. They read these as volunteering, and filing them as clinical is the mistake students find out about at application time.
Log it under Volunteering
Keep it here anyway
Keep it here anyway stays available and is remembered. HQ flags and offers; it never moves your records for you. If your front-desk role does involve patient contact, you know that and HQ doesn't.
🐏
Why this one is on the list at all
Front desk is the single most common thing pre-meds miscount as clinical. Leaving it off the list would not prevent the mistake. It would just mean nobody warns you.

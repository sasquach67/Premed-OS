Why this shrank
The original version classified every experience and showed a verdict panel each time. For an EMT, a scribe, or an MA that just confirms what you already knew, and the real confusion (which job to take) happens months before anything gets logged. What survives is a mis-filing catch, which is smaller and honest about its size.

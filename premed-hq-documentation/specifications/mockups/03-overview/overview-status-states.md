# Overview status and absence states

**Status:** PROPOSED · **Scope:** Where I Stand, Smart next actions, Quick Access, and roadmap absence.

## Behaviour

- One Where I Stand row opens at a time. Its body routes to the pillar; position and cross-pillar attribution links are more specific.
- Estimated blocks remain visible but do not imply measured pace. Domains without a student-set target show no bar.
- Smart Actions has at most three explained recommendations. When the last is dismissed, the entire widget unmounts.
- Quick Access appears only for real destinations. The roadmap empty state routes to milestone setup and never displays generic dates.

## Appearance

- **One treatment only.** This board uses the approved 7/5 bento density and quiet solid cards because these are state contracts, not competing page layouts.
- The expanded row is nested under its parent with compact bordered record rows; the estimated block uses a dashed border instead of a false measurement treatment.
- The Smart Actions absence state is deliberately an open bento gap, shown as its own explanatory panel only for review.

## Deliberately absent

No universal readiness score, no progress bar without a target, no empty Smart Actions shell, no generic roadmap dates, and no launcher for an absent destination.

What this is — and what it deliberately isn't

A toggle in the top bar, not a tab. It opens as an overlay so it's reachable from anywhere and never loses your place. Week is default; Month and Agenda switch inside.

Read-mostly. No event creation, no drag-to-reschedule, no editing in place. Clicking anything deep-links to its owner — the problem set opens in Assignments, CHEM Exam 2 opens exam prep mode, FL3 opens the full-length flow. Editing happens where the thing lives.

HQ's items vs yours are visibly different. Pillar accents for what HQ scheduled; a single muted, dashed treatment for anything imported from Google Calendar. You must be able to tell at a glance, or the view stops being trustworthy.

No new entity. Everything here is a projection over Course meeting times, ClassAssignment deadlines, PlanSession, FullLength, and imported events. If a builder reaches for a CalendarEvent table, that's the wrong turn — it stops being a view and becomes a calendar.

Note the FL3 block on Saturday and its reserved review block on Sunday — that's §3.6's "an FL is a scheduled object" showing up where you'd actually notice it. And the footer's 22 of 34 planned hours is the shared hour budget (§11b) surfacing without a separate screen.

Month is density-first: coloured dots for what kind of thing, small tags for the few items that matter (Exam, FL3, W deadline). Not a wall of text at 1/7th width.

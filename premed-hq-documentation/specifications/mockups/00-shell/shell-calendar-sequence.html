What the sequence settles

Overview owns it, the top bar reaches it. Step 1 shows the week strip as an Overview panel — that's the calendar's home, and it spans every pillar because Overview is the cross-pillar surface. Step 2 shows the same toggle still sitting in the top bar three screens away.

Step 3 is the whole argument for an overlay. Academics is still visible behind the veil. You checked your week without leaving CHEM 261, and step 4b returns you to the exact same scroll position. If the calendar were Overview-only, that glance would cost you two navigations and your place.

Step 4a is the read-mostly rule made concrete. Clicking FL3 doesn't open an edit popover in the calendar — it takes you to the full-length record in MCAT, where rescheduling and logging actually live. The calendar never edits anything.

The two entry points are not redundant. The Overview panel answers "how is my week shaped" when you're already surveying. The toggle answers "wait, when is that" mid-task. Different questions, same data, one component.

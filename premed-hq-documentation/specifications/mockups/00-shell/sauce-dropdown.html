What the scope-down cut. Earlier drafts had two doors — a full-width block on Overview and a feed page at /overview/sauce with filters, history, and saved items. The feed page is gone. A browsable archive of pre-med knowledge is Atlas, and building one inside HQ duplicates the product this is meant to be a window into. Andy: “to build this would essentially be to replace Atlas and I'm not tryna do that.” So HQ shows three and points out — “Open in Atlas →” is the only exit, and Atlas owns browsing.

What it costs, honestly. The original bar was “all it takes is for it to be there, and people just look at it.” A dropdown is not ambient — it needs a click. The labelled pill is the mitigation: the word Sauce stays visible even closed, so the thing is discoverable without occupying page space. If it turns out nobody opens it, that is the trade failing, and the answer would be one ambient line in the top bar rather than a bento block.

Why it is a pill and not an icon. The bell means things that need you. Sauce means things you might like. Two adjacent bell-shaped icons would make Sauce inherit obligation, and obligation is what kills it. Different shape, different side of the search field, and never a badge, count, or dot.

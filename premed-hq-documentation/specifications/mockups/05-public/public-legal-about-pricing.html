A note from the creator.

What's up! I'm Andy, a Neuroscience major on the pre-med track here at UNC-Chapel Hill. I thought of this app in the beginning of summer and I've been working on it throughout, and this is my first big project! As a pre-med interested in AI, this was a really fun experience in terms of designing, building, and iterating my first ever app, or website, or whatever you want to call it.

Initially, I was tracking things like my graduation requirements and experience hours using Google Sheets, and so were a lot of my peers. I figured: "why not have a tracker where everything is in one place?" I used other apps like Calendar and Notion to track my classes, but using all of these apps felt a bit convoluted.

So I built HQ to put everything in one place, and to hopefully make OUR pre-med journey a tiny bit easier. My noob self has been the only developer of this app, so I'm open to any form of feedback or advice from others!

Privacy

What's stored, what leaves your device, who receives it, and how to get all of it back or destroy it.

Last updated 8 August 2026

Local first, really

In short

HQ works with no account. Until you make one, everything you type lives in your browser and nowhere else.

Your records are written to local storage on your device as you enter them. Signing in adds sync across devices and a server copy. It does not change what the app can do — there is no feature behind the account, and the signed-out experience is the whole product.

What leaves your device

In short

Only the specific things listed below, only for the feature that needs them, and several have a local-only alternative.

WhatWhenLocal-only option
Your records — courses, hours, notesOnly if you create an account, to syncYes — stay signed out
Coursework text for AI featuresWhen you use a feature that reads itYes — those features have deterministic fallbacks
Lecture audioOnly if you record a lectureYes — transcription runs on your device by default
Canvas assignment dataOnly if you connect CanvasYes — don't connect it

On-device transcription is the default, not an option you have to find. Lecture audio never has to leave your machine.

Who receives it

In short

Three processors, named, each with what it gets. No one else.

ProcessorReceives
SupabaseYour account and your synced records, if you have an account
The AI providerOnly the text a feature needs, at the moment you use it. Never your whole workspace
The transcription providerNothing, unless you turn off on-device transcription

What we don't do

In short

No third-party analytics, no advertising technology, no sale of data, and no training on your work.

  • No analytics SDKs. Usage instrumentation exists so features can be judged, and it is local and yours — you can see it and clear it.
  • No ad tech, no trackers, no fingerprinting.
  • Your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • Your content is not used to train external models.

Shared syllabus parses

In short

When you share a parsed syllabus, it is structurally impossible for your grades or progress to travel with it.

A shared parse contains dates, units, weights, and policies from the professor's document. It lives in a separate store with no join path to your records and an allow-list of fields. The safety here is architectural, not a promise about our care. The source document itself is never shared.

Patient information

In short

Don't put identifiable patient details in reflections. HQ will quietly say so if it spots something, and will never block you from saving.

Clinical, shadowing, and volunteering reflections are free text. Write about what you learned, not who you saw — no names, dates of birth, room numbers, or record numbers. HQ is not a compliance product and does not claim to be.

Keeping, exporting, deleting

In short

Export any time, in one click. Delete your account and the server copy goes, on a stated timetable, without emailing anyone.

  • Export is a button in Settings and produces a complete, readable file.
  • Deletion is self-serve. Server data is removed within 30 days, backups roll off within 90.
  • Local data is yours to keep or clear — deleting the account doesn't reach into your browser.
  • Disconnecting an integration never deletes records that came in through it.

Changes

In short

Material changes are dated and listed here, not silently swapped in.

Terms of use

What you can expect from HQ, what HQ expects from you, and the limits on both.

Last updated 8 August 2026

Beta status

In short

This is a public beta run by one student. There is no uptime guarantee. Keep an exported copy of anything that matters.

Features may change or be removed. Your data is local-first specifically so an outage cannot separate you from your own records, and export exists so you're never dependent on this service continuing.

Numbers are estimates

In short

Every number HQ produces is an estimate to verify. Do not submit an application on the strength of one.

GPA, BCPM classification, grade projections, hour totals, readiness reads, and score bands are computed from what you enter and from published rules that change between cycles. Verify against your official transcript, AMCAS, and your pre-health advisor. HQ is not academic, medical, legal, or financial advice.

Your content

In short

You own everything you write. HQ gets only the permission needed to store it and show it back to you.

  • You keep ownership of your notes, reflections, essays, and records.
  • HQ receives a limited licence to store, process, and display your content to you, for the purpose of operating the service, and for nothing else.
  • The licence ends when you delete. It does not survive your account.

Acceptable use

In short

Don't use HQ to distribute material you don't have the right to distribute, and don't attack the service.

  • Past exams and course materials: every shared entry carries a permission status, and material of unknown origin is never shared. HQ is not a distribution network.
  • Syllabi: sharing a parse shares structure and facts. Do not use HQ to redistribute the document itself.
  • Lecture recordings: your instructor's policy is yours to observe. Where an instructor posts their own recording, use that.
  • Conversations: get consent before recording another person.
  • No scraping, no automated bulk access, no attempts to bypass rate limits or per-user budgets.

Other services

In short

Canvas, Google, Anki, and any prep provider have their own terms, and those are between you and them.

HQ links to and integrates with third-party services at your direction. Canvas access is read-only, permanently — HQ never submits work, posts, or writes on your behalf. No integration with paid answer-sharing services will ever be built.

Community content

In short

Advice gathered from forums is opinion, attributed and dated. It is not official guidance and sometimes it's wrong.

Where community consensus conflicts with the AAMC or a university, HQ shows both and says which is which. Posts are summarised and linked, never republished.

Limits of liability

In short

HQ is provided as is. It is not liable for admissions outcomes or decisions made from its numbers.

To the extent the law allows, Premed HQ is provided without warranties and is not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including any application outcome. Nothing here limits rights that cannot be limited.

Changes

In short

Material changes get a dated entry and notice in the app before they take effect.

Pricing It's free for now, don't worry.
Everything, free, during the beta

There's no paid plan, no card on file, and nothing here to buy. Every tracker, every plan, every calculation, and the AI features are all just on.

Most of HQ costs nothing to run. The parts that cost money are the AI ones, and I'd rather measure a full semester of real usage than guess a number now and get it wrong in either direction. When there is a real price, it'll be on this page, and you'll know long before anything changes.

Your records are never paywalled. If there's ever a paid tier and you don't want it, you keep everything you've logged and you can still export it.
Export and deletion stay free and self-serve, always, and never require emailing anyone.
Any paid tier will state its cap. "Unlimited AI" at a student price is a promise nobody can keep, so I won't make it.
No ads, no affiliate links, no sponsored placement, on either side of a paywall. Free options get named first.

Curious, or want to argue about what it should cost? Tell me.

What these four are testing

One shell, four pages. Same pill nav, same 720px measure, same title band, same footer. A legal page that looks like a different site reads as boilerplate someone pasted in, and boilerplate is exactly what these must not be.

Every section opens with a plain-English summary. 05 §6.4 requires plain language; a tinted one-sentence strip above each section is how that gets enforced structurally rather than hoped for. If a summary and its section ever disagree, the section is wrong.

Privacy names the processors and the local-only alternatives in a table, because that is the part people actually want and the part most policies bury. On-device transcription is called out separately — it is both the biggest cost decision in the product and the strongest privacy claim it can make.

Pricing is a real page behind a real nav item. Andy asked for a button that doesn't work yet; a dead button is a defect, so the nav works and lands here, and the only inactive control is a clearly disabled No paid plan yet. No plan cards, no fake tiers, no "coming soon" table with blurred numbers.

Still to resolve (05 §10): the age floor for Terms, governing law, and the Premed HQ trademark and domain check. All three are blockers for publishing these pages, not for designing them.

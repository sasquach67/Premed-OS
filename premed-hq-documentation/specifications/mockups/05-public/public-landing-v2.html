What changed, and why. This mockup is direction, not pixel law — the deliverable is the structure and the single token block.

1 · The three floating islands STAY. The bug was placement, not the pattern. justify-content: space-between centres the pill between its neighbours, not on the page — and since "Premed HQ" and "Start tracking ↗" are different widths, the pill sits off-centre by half that difference, getting worse as the rail widens. Now a 1fr auto 1fr grid, so the pill is on the true optical centre at every width. The pill holds only the five links.
2 · The brand is a two-tone wordmark, no icon. All six references share that format, and Premed OS makes it automatic — "OS" is the second tone. Four options above; C is shown live. ⚠️ The references are geometric sans; Baloo 2 is rounded and never changes, so the format transfers but the letterforms stay warmer. Klinar is the closest of the six to what Baloo 2 can do.
3 · The top-right CTA is coloured glass — saturated tint, luminous top edge, and an outer bloom in the same hue. The bloom is the whole trick: every other glass surface here has the inset highlight and stops there, so this is the only element that appears to emit light rather than transmit it. ⚠️ The hero CTA stays solid white and stays the primary — colour attracts, but on a dark field white outweighs it. Two competing primaries was the original problem; this is a second-place accent, not a tie.
4 · The gooey filter is gone. filter: url(#hq-gooey) made a containing block and clipped; a transform slides the arrow just as well.
5 · Hero is five blocks, not six, and the type is much bigger — the payoff line tracks 7.4vw, so it lands ~142px at 1920px and the words span the measure instead of floating in it. The hero gets a wider rail than the tile grid: display type and a 5-column grid want different measures.
6 · Vertical rhythm opened up. "No account needed" was sitting almost against the buttons, so it read as a caption on the CTA instead of a separate promise. Because the hero is 100svh, any space the stack doesn't use becomes dead air above and below it — spreading the blocks fills the screen rather than centring a tight clump in it.
7 · Tile interior spacing, and the gaps are uneven on purpose. SEE HOW gets more room under the title than the title gets under the icon — it's an affordance, not content, and equal gaps made it read as a third line of the title. The title also flexes, so SEE HOW sits on the tile floor and every affordance in a row lines up whether its title wraps to two lines or three.
8 · The bottom row exists now. It was missing and you caught it. The footer carries the long form of the independence and AAMC disclaimers — the hero has the one-line version, the footer has the full one (05 §6.1, §6.2). ⚠️ Never reveal-gated, never animated: it got stranded behind the scroll observer once, and a required legal line at opacity 0 is a compliance problem rather than a visual one.
9 · Every property declared once. .pl-hl-hero .l2 font-size was set 5 times in the live CSS and .pl-hero-in 6 times; the max-width:1100px and 900px breakpoints were dead, overridden by later non-media rules. That cascade is why your edits scrambled the type.

Not in this mockup: the guided tour section, which sits between Features and the footer. It is unchanged by this pass — see the notes on screenshots.

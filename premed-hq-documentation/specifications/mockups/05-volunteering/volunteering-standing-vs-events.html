You came back to the Turkey Trot

Two years running with Chapel Hill Parks. Want to track this as a standing commitment instead? It keeps both sessions and starts showing longevity.

Why two shapes, and not one. Andy: “in Clinical you have roles that you stick with. In Volunteering you can have those same roles, but there are more temporary ones that are kind of one-off.” Clinical inherited a hero of one card per site because every clinical record is a relationship. Volunteering has both kinds, so it needs both shapes. A card answers “what is my relationship here.” A row answers “what did I do that day.” Forcing a Turkey Trot into a card produces a mostly-empty card, and eight of them read as eight abandoned commitments regardless of the copy.

The events panel carries a real total — 34 hours, six events — in the same weight the cards use for theirs. It is not a footnote under the real work. And “longest 18 mo” in the banner reads across standing commitments only: that number sitting beside a list of events was the specific thing V-12's copy rule could not fix on its own.

The promotion is an offer, never automatic (#12–15's flag-and-offer rule), and the test is deterministic — a second session, nothing more. No AI, no sourced threshold. It rewards returning without ever scolding the student who did not.

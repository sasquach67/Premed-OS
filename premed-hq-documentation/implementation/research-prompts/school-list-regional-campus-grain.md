# Research packet — School List: regional-campus roster grain

**Question.** How should the roster understand Sidney Kimmel Medical College's Delaware regional campus and Tufts' Maine Track: as independently selectable applicant options, separately accredited programs, or regional-campus variants of a parent MD program?

**Scope.** This records current school, AAMC, and accreditation evidence. It does **not** decide whether either roster row stays, changes a displayed count, or edits the shipped directory.

**Accessed:** 2026-08-13

## Primary-source evidence

### Tufts Maine Track

- [Tufts — secondary application](https://medicine.tufts.edu/admissions-financial-aid/admission-program/doctor-medicine/secondary-application) — says an applicant who designated Tufts on AMCAS can use the secondary site to submit both the MD application and a Maine Track application. Accessed 2026-08-13.
- [Tufts — apply to the Maine Track MD program](https://medicine.tufts.edu/admissions-financial-aid/admission-program/doctor-medicine/maine-track-md-program) — says Maine Track applicants are reviewed for two tracks; identifies Maine Medical Center in **Portland, ME** as the Maine Track interview site; says most of the first year is in Boston, followed by most of the experience in Maine. Accessed 2026-08-13.
- [Tufts — Maine Track MD](https://medicine.tufts.edu/academics/medicine/maine-track-md) — says Maine Track is developed with MaineHealth, is part of Tufts University School of Medicine, and spends the majority of its time in Maine. Accessed 2026-08-13.
- [Tufts — accreditation](https://medicine.tufts.edu/about/school-medicine/about-school-medicine/accreditation) — says LCME continued full accreditation of the Tufts medical education program for an eight-year term in 2022. Accessed 2026-08-13.

### Sidney Kimmel Delaware Regional Campus

- [Jefferson — Delaware Regional Campus associate-dean posting](https://jeffersonhealth.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/ThomasJeffersonExternal/job/Philadelphia-PA/Associate-Dean--Delaware-Regional-Campus-of-the-Sidney-Kimmel-Medical-College--SKMC-_REQ-0034438-1) — Jefferson's first-party posting says Delaware selected Jefferson to open an SKMC regional campus; it identifies University of Delaware in **Newark** as the inaugural-class location and says the class is planned to matriculate in fall 2028. Accessed 2026-08-13.
- [Jefferson — Dean's Column, summer 2026](https://www.jefferson.edu/alumni/connect/alumni-bulletin/summer-2026/deans-column.html) — describes the Delaware program as the state's first medical school and says Jefferson planned at least 40 students enrolled by fall 2028. Accessed 2026-08-13.

### AAMC / LCME regional-campus context

- [AAMC — Regional Medical Campuses in North America](https://www.aamc.org/learn-network/affinity-groups/medical-education-leadership-administration-group/regional-medical-campuses-north-america) — defines a regional campus as an instructional site distinct from the central/administrative campus where some students spend one or more complete curricular years. It lists Tufts with Maine Medical Center and numerous other parent-school/regional-campus pairs, including UAB Tuscaloosa/Montgomery/Huntsville, Texas A&M's several campuses, Texas Tech's Permian Basin/Amarillo sites, and UCSF Berkeley. Accessed 2026-08-13.
- [LCME directory](https://lcme.org/directory/accredited-u-s-programs/) — authoritative directory route for accredited U.S. MD programs. This pass did not locate a separately named current entry for either the Tufts Maine Track or the future Delaware campus. **That is a search non-finding, not proof of absence.** Accessed 2026-08-13.

## What this establishes—and what it does not

1. **Tufts Maine Track is applicant-facing and separately selectable inside Tufts' admissions flow.** It is therefore materially different from a generic clinical site, even though the evidence identifies it as a track within the parent MD program.
2. **The Delaware entry is time-sensitive.** Current first-party information describes a program expected to begin in 2028, so a directory must not make it look like a current-cycle applicant can select it unless a current application path is independently verified.
3. **This is a systemic grain question, not just two odd records.** AAMC's regional-campus inventory lists many such instructional sites. A roster that mixes accreditation-program grain and applicant-selectable regional-option grain needs an explicit standard and a current-cycle effective date.

## Evidence-backed implications — non-binding

1. A future directory field could distinguish `parentProgram`, `regionalTrack`, `applicationPath`, `location`, and `availableFromCycle`, instead of forcing every entry to look like an independently accredited school.
2. The user-facing question for inclusion should be “can an applicant select this current-cycle option?” rather than “does LCME list a separate medical school?” That is an inference about product grain, **not a decided rule**.
3. Any future map/display must use the stated instructional or interview location and a cycle-effective status; it must not infer a city from the parent institution.

## Explicit non-findings and limits

- No current AMCAS record was found in this pass documenting a separate primary-application designation for either the Maine Track or the future Delaware campus.
- No separate LCME accreditation record for either site was located in this pass. The Tufts evidence supports parent-program accreditation; the Delaware campus is not yet operating.
- This packet does not set a rule for the many other regional campuses AAMC lists, does not change the two existing roster rows, and does not change any displayed roster count.

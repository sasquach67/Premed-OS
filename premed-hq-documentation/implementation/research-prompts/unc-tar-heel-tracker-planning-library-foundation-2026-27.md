# Research packet — UNC Tar Heel Tracker planning-library foundation (2026–2027)

**Status:** research and specification foundation only; no product, mockup, requirements-JSON, or planning-UI change is authorized by this document.  
**Retrieved:** 2026-08-25. **Catalog edition:** 2026–2027 unless a source explicitly states that an admission cohort governs.  
**Discovery and validation:** Exa was used for four discovery workstreams (50 surfaced results); every retained program row below was then checked on `catalog.unc.edu` or its owning UNC page. Search results themselves are not evidence.

## Product boundary

This library can present catalog-derived planning requirements for a selected **requirement term + program + degree + track/concentration + admission state**. It must never call itself the student's official audit, declare graduation clearance, infer substitutions, or replace the student's ConnectCarolina Tar Heel Tracker. UNC describes that Tracker as the degree-audit tool and says a requirement term identifies the catalog year for a student's requirements; different majors/minors can have different terms. [Registrar guide](https://registrar.unc.edu/tar-heel-tracker-student-guide/) · [catalog scope and cohort rule](https://catalog.unc.edu/undergraduate/).

### 2026–27 IDEAs node set

The 2026–27 page's requirements are specifically for students beginning fall 2026; fall 2022 through spring 2026 students must use the appropriate archived edition. It requires First-Year Foundations (IDST 101, IDST 111L, First-Year Seminar/Launch, ENGL 105, Global Language through level 3); nine 3-credit Focus Capacities plus one 1-credit Empirical Investigation Lab; Research and Discovery; High-Impact Experience or second R&D (never the same course); Communication Beyond Carolina; Interdisciplinary; Lifetime Fitness; Campus Life Experience (eight events/four categories); Foundations of American Democracy; and three-division distribution. B.A. degrees also need Supplemental General Education. CAA and first-year early-college exceptions are explicitly scoped. Professional B.S. exceptions can reduce Focus Capacities. These are **attribute- and cohort-dependent nodes**, not a flat credit total. [IDEAs in Action](https://catalog.unc.edu/undergraduate/ideas-in-action/).

Automate only a catalog course's recorded attributes after retaining their source term; leave CLE attendance, language placement/credit, petitions, CAA qualification, course attributes at enrollment, substitutions, and professional-school reductions as official-audit/adviser-confirmed states.

## 2. Normalized library schema (specification input, not JSON)

```text
ProgramRequirementSet
  id; catalogYear; requirementTermPolicy; school; program; degree; trackOrConcentration
  sourceUrl; retrievedAt; sourceOwner; admissionGate; totalHoursPublished
  globalConstraints[]; requirementNodes[]; manualReviewFlags[]; coverageStatus

RequirementNode
  id; kind (course|all_of|choose_n|minimum_credits|range|attribute|residency|capstone|gate)
  label; credits {exact|min|max}; members[]; count; subjectOrLevelRule
  exclusions[]; noDoubleCountWith[]; gradeMinimum; sequencing[]; sourceAnchor
  completionMode (catalog-evaluable|official-audit-only|advisor-confirmation)
```

The implementation must retain **both** a human-readable rule and machine-safe predicates. `choose_n` options, course ranges, elective lists, exclusions, footnotes, prerequisites, and no-double-count clauses must stay separate nodes; no generic "science elective" flattening. A course can satisfy multiple catalog nodes only when an explicit policy says so.

## 4. Explicit exclusions

- **Minors, certificates, graduate/dual degrees, and every unrelated undergraduate major** are outside this first declared pre-health/STEM planning universe. They are not "missing graduation requirements"; they need their own scoped packet.
- **Medical-school prerequisites** are excluded: they vary by applicant school and do not equal UNC major requirements.
- **Individual credit and exception data** are excluded from the library: transfer/AP/IB articulation, waived/placed-out requirements, petitions, substitutions, repeat/grade treatment, registration availability, CLE attendance, and adviser/program approvals must be imported as official or user-supplied evidence and visibly labeled.
- **A generic “Public Health” record is prohibited.** The five B.S.P.H. majors and their distinct tracks/concentrations are separate records.

# Research packet — School List `SL-21`: cycle calendar and phase gate

**Question.** Can Premed OS derive the School List cycle gate from a student's expected application year, or must the student explicitly enter application mode?

**Scope.** AMCAS (AAMC), AACOMAS (AACOM), and TMDSAS official sources only. This is evidence for a later product ruling, **not** a product ruling. It does not authorize hard-coding dates, changing the phase gate, or adding calendar data to the app.

## Date stability — what the evidence supports

### AMCAS

The official 2024, 2025, and 2027 materials show the same broad rhythm but not a fixed date:

| Cycle | Opens | Submission | Initial transmission |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 2024 | May 6 | May 28 | June 28 |
| 2025 | May 1 | May 27 | June 27 |
| 2027 | May 5 | May 28 | June 26 |

**Evidence-backed reading:** AMCAS is predictably early May / late May / late June, but its own published dates move by weekday and year. A derivation rule would be an inference, not an official calendar.

### AACOMAS

The two current/historical official fact sheets establish a first-Monday-in-May-looking pattern (May 5 in 2025–26; May 4 in 2026–27), but that is **only two cycles**. AACOM's public page does not publish a three-cycle history or a rule.

### TMDSAS

The 2026 and 2027 official pages show the same May 1 availability / May 15 submission pattern. That is **two cycles**, not the requested three. No official three-cycle historical calendar or machine-readable feed was found in this pass.

## Machine-readable access

**Non-finding.** No official JSON, CSV, or ICS feed was found for AMCAS, AACOMAS, or TMDSAS cycle dates. The sources located are HTML pages, PDFs, and official webinar material. This is a non-finding, not proof that no private or portal-only API exists.

## Evidence-backed implications — non-binding

1. An expected application cycle can support an **orientation cue** such as “the next cycle usually opens in early May,” but the app should not present a calculated service date as authoritative without a current official lookup.
2. The safest candidate for the actual `SL-21` gate is an **explicit student-controlled application-mode switch**, optionally informed by their expected cycle. This avoids a UI suddenly turning on because of a date the app derived from incomplete history.
3. If the product later displays service dates, it needs a freshness-labelled annual data update rather than a permanent date formula. TMDSAS's ranking consequence is especially unsuitable for approximation.
4. Service-level calendar facts and school-level deadline facts remain distinct: AMCAS and AACOM both direct applicants to a separate program/deadline source, and no service-wide “latest school deadline” exists.

## Explicit non-findings and limits

- This packet did **not** find three consecutive official cycles for AACOMAS or TMDSAS, so it cannot claim a stable hard-coded rule for either service.
- The official public AACOMAS and TMDSAS sources examined did not state a general verification turnaround or separate first-transmission date for their current cycles.
- The packet does **not** choose the `SL-21` gate, add a calendar feature, populate the roster, or change any localStorage shape.
- Exact dates remain current-cycle facts and may change after this access date.

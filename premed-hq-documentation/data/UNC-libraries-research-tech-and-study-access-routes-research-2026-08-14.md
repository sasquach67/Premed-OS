# UNC libraries, research, technology, and study-access routes

**Corpus:** Atlas Research Corpus / UNC official-resource backbone  
**Scope:** Owner routes for a student who needs research help, a place to work, materials, data support, technology, citation help, or an accessibility accommodation.  
**Checked:** 2026-08-14  
**Evidence standard:** First-party UNC Library and UNC IT pages only. This is a routing packet, not a claim that a particular room, appointment, item, software license, or service slot is available.

## How Atlas should use this packet

Route the student by the *immediate task*, then send them to the resource owner. The display should distinguish:

`owner route exists` → `student opens current page / signs in if required` → `live page determines availability, eligibility, rules, and turnaround`.

Do **not** translate any route below into a promise that a student can reserve a space, borrow a device, access a database from off campus, receive a consultation, or use paid software.

## Research and assignment support

### Starting a project or getting unstuck

UNC Library’s [Research Support](https://library.unc.edu/research-support/) and [Research Guides](https://library.unc.edu/research-guides/) are the canonical discovery routes. The guide layer is the efficient first stop when a question is discipline-, source-type-, or citation-specific; the support layer is the owner route when a student needs help forming a search strategy, locating material, or navigating a research process.

For a consultation, use the Library’s [scheduling page](https://library.unc.edu/schedule-research-consultations/). This packet intentionally does not state which appointments are open, who is eligible, or which specialties are currently offered. Those are live-service conditions.

**Atlas routing language:**

> Need help finding or evaluating sources? Start with the UNC Library research guide for your topic. If the question is still specific to your project, use the Library’s live consultation scheduler rather than guessing which office owns it.

### Requests that look like research help but need a different owner

- **A required course reading:** check [Course Reserves](https://library.unc.edu/borrow/course-reserves/) before treating it as a general library-search problem.
- **A missing book/article:** start at [Borrow](https://library.unc.edu/borrow/) and the current interlibrary-loan route. Timing is not guaranteed.
- **A course content question, grading issue, or tutoring need:** this packet does not replace the instructor/TA or the academic-support routes documented separately.
- **A special-collections request:** use the collection’s own access/request page; public listings do not establish on-site access or retrieval time.

## Study spaces and access

### Find the *right kind* of space before trying to reserve it

The Library’s [space directory](https://library.unc.edu/spaces/) is the source of truth for library spaces and their listed characteristics. It is the correct front door for comparing group work, quieter study, media/production, special equipment, and location-specific facilities.

**Verification boundary:** individual library/room pages and the live reservation interface, where present, govern hours, access requirements, capacity, conduct rules, and booking status. Atlas should say “check this space’s live page,” not “go to this room” or “reserve this room.”

### Library entry and borrowing

Use the Library’s [Borrow](https://library.unc.edu/borrow/) section for circulation, accounts, requests, and borrowing rules. [My Library Accounts](https://library.unc.edu/borrow/my-library-accounts/) is the account-management route. UNC’s [Carolina BLU interlibrary-loan FAQ](https://library.unc.edu/borrow/carolina-blu/interlibrary-loan-faq/) is a separate owner source for sharing/request questions.

Do not infer patron status, borrowing duration, renewability, fees, delivery time, remote-database access, or material eligibility from a general UNC affiliation. Those vary by material and live policy.

## Data, GIS, and reproducible research

Library Data Services explicitly owns a structured set of routes for [finding/managing data](https://library.unc.edu/data/finding-managing-data/), [research/analysis](https://library.unc.edu/data/research-analysis/), [spatial analysis/GIS](https://library.unc.edu/data/research-analysis/spatial-analysis/), and [data visualization](https://library.unc.edu/data/storytelling-visualization/data-visualization/). The Library also maintains a [Data Services desk](https://library.unc.edu/data/service-desk/) and [data consultation calendar](https://calendar.lib.unc.edu/appointments/data), both of which need live availability checks.

**Good Atlas scenario prompts:**

- “I have a dataset but do not know how to clean or document it.” → Data Services research/analysis route.
- “I need a map or location-based analysis.” → spatial/GIS route.
- “I need data for a paper and am unsure whether I can use it.” → finding/managing data route first; then verify license, privacy, and method requirements.
- “I need help explaining a visualization.” → visualization and data-storytelling route.

**Hard boundary:** a data-support route does not approve human-subjects work, protected-data access, statistical conclusions, or responsible/reproducible use. Atlas must route those questions to the relevant research-compliance, faculty, or course owner when applicable.

## Technology, making, and media

### Computers, printing, scanning

[Printers, scanners, and computers](https://library.unc.edu/printers-scanners-computers/) is the library owner route. It should be surfaced for a student whose need is operational (“where can I scan this?”), not as a promise of current hardware, printing availability, accessibility compatibility, or a particular price.

### Equipment and production

The Library separates general [equipment borrowing](https://library.unc.edu/borrow/borrow-equipment/) from Media Design Center [equipment](https://library.unc.edu/media-design-center/view-media-equipment/) and [film/space booking](https://library.unc.edu/media-design-center/book-media-design-center-films-and-spaces/). The [Makerspace](https://library.unc.edu/makerspace/) owns its own service route and, for specific fabrication requests, publishes a [3D-printing form](https://library.unc.edu/makerspace/3d-printing-form/).

Present these as *routes to inspect*: stock, checkout rules, training, reservation systems, turnaround, and eligibility are all mutable.

### Software and central technology support

For data-specific tools, begin with [Data Services Software](https://library.unc.edu/data/data-services-software/). For university-wide software discovery, use the official [UNC software catalog](https://software.sites.unc.edu/software/). For an account, device, network, or general technology incident, use the central [UNC Help](https://help.unc.edu/) portal.

This separation matters: an application mentioned in a course, a library guide, or an old student post is not proof that it remains UNC-licensed or personally installable today.

## Citation and writing workflow

The Library’s [citation guide index](https://library.unc.edu/research-guides/citing/) owns citation-tool and citation-style routing; its [Zotero guide](https://library.unc.edu/research-guides/citing/zotero/) is the focused entry point for Zotero. It also publishes entries for [EndNote](https://library.unc.edu/research-guides/citing/endnote/) and [SciWheel](https://library.unc.edu/research-guides/citing/sciwheel/).

**Safe workflow to show students:**

`course/instructor citation requirement` → `choose the matching Library guide/tool route` → `import/manage sources` → `manually verify author, title, date, capitalization, page/DOI, and final style`

Do not frame tool output as automatically correct. The student or instructor’s stated style requirement controls.

## Accessibility and equipment route

When the barrier is a library resource or digital material, surface the Library’s [accessibility remediation request](https://library.unc.edu/accessibility-remediation-request/). It is a request path, not a guarantee of an immediate alternate format or a replacement for disability-accommodation processes. For access questions about a physical location or device, the relevant live space/equipment page is still needed.

## Research gaps / follow-up rules

- A future live-data layer may capture current hours, location attributes, and reservation URLs only with a dated fetch and a clear expiration policy.
- Do not use this packet to decide the best library space, a student’s eligibility for an item, whether software works on a device, or whether a data project is methodologically/ethically permissible.
- Pair this official packet with a later, clearly labeled student-experience layer before making comfort, workload, crowding, or “best place to study” recommendations.

# UNC career development and internships: safe routing rules

**Research date:** 2026-08-14 (America/New_York)  
**Scope:** Current first-party UNC Career Center material, supplemented by a historical UCS policy only to describe platform provenance. This is a routing packet—not a directory of openings, an employer endorsement, or an outcome guarantee.

## Decision in one screen

For a UNC student who needs help exploring careers, finding an internship, preparing an application, meeting employers, or talking with alumni, the current owner is the [UNC Career Center student hub](https://careers.unc.edu/students/). Its live ecosystem separates into:

| Student need | Start here | What to do next | Do not assume |
|---|---|---|---|
| I do not know what career/major direction fits me | [Career Center student hub](https://careers.unc.edu/students/) | Use the current class-year resources, Focus2 link, and career-exploration programming; book coaching if the question needs individual context. | That an assessment chooses a major or career for the student. |
| I need a job or internship lead | [Handshake](https://careers.unc.edu/resource/handshake/) | Complete/update the profile, inspect the live listing, and apply under the employer's stated process. | That a listing is open, suited to pre-health, paid, vetted for every purpose, or schedule-compatible. |
| I need résumé, cover-letter, interview, or search feedback | [Career coaching / appointment route](https://careers.unc.edu/students/schedule-an-appointment/) | Check current drop-in/appointment availability; bring the actual target role or document. | That feedback guarantees an interview or offer. |
| I want to meet employers or learn what a field is like | [Career Center events calendar](https://careerhub.unc.edu/) | Filter/read the current event detail; RSVP and verify format, audience, deadline, and any preparation. | That attending creates a referral, interview, or relationship. |
| I want an alumni perspective or connection | [Heels Engage](https://careers.unc.edu/resources/heels-engage-network/) | Use the UNC-linked alumni networking route; make a narrow, respectful informational request. | That an alumnus owes a response, referral, job, or shadowing access. |
| Cost could block professional clothing or interview travel | [Career Center student hub](https://careers.unc.edu/students/) | Check the current funding/program page and its eligibility, deadline, and available balance. | That funds are open, automatic, or enough for a particular expense. |

## What the official career system offers

### The Career Center is a preparation and navigation owner

The current Student hub says the Career Center can coach students on locating part-time/full-time work and internships, preparing for employers, and connecting academic interests with possible career paths. It points students to a live calendar, coaching appointments/drop-ins, year-specific starting points, and tools such as Handshake. The site states that its calendar has 100+ workshops, career fairs, and employer recruiting events per semester; that is a description of the program, **not** a promise that a specific event will exist or fit a particular student. [UNC Career Center: Students](https://careers.unc.edu/students/)

**Useful first-year sequence:**

`explore interests/values` → `make a basic résumé` → `complete Handshake` → `attend one low-stakes workshop or employer event` → `use targeted coaching once a role or direction is concrete`.

That sequence is an Atlas navigation recommendation inferred from the service design. It is not evidence that every student should pursue an internship in a particular year, field, or timeline.

### Handshake is the live opportunity and event surface

UNC directs students to Handshake for campus-job searching, workshop registration, and employer events, and describes it as the place to look for postings and connections tailored to a student's interests once a profile is completed. The dedicated route states that current degree-seeking students access it using a valid ONYEN. Individual records are dynamic/authenticated and must be read on the owner platform at the point of use. [UNC Career Center: Students](https://careers.unc.edu/students/) · [UNC Handshake route](https://careers.unc.edu/resource/handshake/)

Use an actual listing to verify:

- employer and work location;
- exact duties, credential requirements, compensation, and weekly-hour expectations;
- application method and deadline;
- whether an event is virtual/in-person, RSVP-limited, or intended for a particular audience;
- whether an opportunity is employment, volunteer work, research, a fellowship, or a short project.

Do **not** let a broad title (for example, “medical,” “health,” “research,” or “assistant”) stand in for verified clinical exposure, patient contact, training quality, work authorization, academic credit, or pre-med value.

### Employer-facing platform rules are provenance, not a safety certification

The most recent publicly indexed UCS recruiting-policy PDF located in this pass is dated 2023–24. It says employer postings were reviewed by UCS and could include full-time, part-time, seasonal, internship, on-campus non-work-study, volunteer, and fellowship categories; it also names categories UCS would not accept. This supports describing Handshake as an institution-managed platform. Because the document is historical, Atlas must **not** present its detailed screening rules as a current guarantee or claim that every current listing is safe, legal, paid, relevant, or right for a particular student. [UCS recruiting policies, 2023–24](https://careers.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/UCS-RECRUITING-POLICIES-PROCEDURES-FOR-Fall-2023_SPRING-2024-_UPDATED_May_15_2023.pdf)

### Networking belongs beside—not instead of—an actual application process

UNC's student page links **Heels Engage** as a UNC-specific alumni-professional network and recommends it for asking alumni questions and hearing how others navigated majors/careers. A useful Atlas prompt can help a student turn a vague request into a respectful, bounded conversation:

`specific shared context` + `one 15–20 minute informational question` + `no request for a job/referral in the first message`.

This is communication guidance, not a statement of any alumnus's obligation or a prediction of response. [UNC Heels Engage](https://careers.unc.edu/resources/heels-engage-network/)

## Scenario playbook

| Situation | Practical route | Ask / prepare | Boundaries Atlas should show |
|---|---|---|---|
| “I am new and do not even know what to search for.” | Career Center class-year resources; Focus2; a career-exploration workshop. | Interests, values, skills, constraints, and a few classes/activities already tried. | Exploration tools organize reflection; they do not diagnose fit or decide a major. |
| “I need a campus job this term.” | Handshake + the separate work-study/aid route if relevant. | Current availability, role duties, hours, pay, start date, and funding mechanism. | A work-study award does not create a job; a listing does not establish eligibility. |
| “I want a summer internship.” | Handshake, employer sites, current Career Center calendar/coach. | Term, location, paid/unpaid requirement, eligibility, work authorization, and application materials. | Do not treat an old calendar date or employer profile as an opening. |
| “Can you look at my résumé before I apply?” | [Career Center appointment/drop-in](https://careers.unc.edu/students/schedule-an-appointment/) or current résumé resources. | The exact role/field and a truthful draft. | No service can promise an interview; never inflate facts, GPA, graduation date, or authorization. |
| “I want to practice interviewing.” | Career Center's current interview-preparation route/workshops and [Big Interview](https://careers.unc.edu/resources/big-interview/). | Role description, examples from real experience, and questions to rehearse. | Practice output is not an employer assessment and should not be represented as one. |
| “I am looking for research.” | [Office for Undergraduate Research opportunities](https://our.unc.edu/find/opportunities/) first; use Career Center support for documents/search strategy where applicable. | Paid vs. credit vs. volunteer, term, skills, time, and lab/program eligibility. | A research profile, department page, or faculty name is not a current vacancy. |
| “I need pre-health guidance.” | [Health Professions Advising](https://prehealth.unc.edu/) for professional-school pathway questions; Career Center for general career search/application skills. | Whether the question is medical-school planning versus a job/internship-search problem. | Do not substitute generalized career coaching for an admissions policy or vice versa. |
| “I am an international student.” | [International Student and Scholar Services](https://isss.unc.edu/) before accepting or beginning employment. | Individual status and exact work arrangement. | A Handshake listing, remote role, unpaid experience, or short project is not automatic authorization. |

## Product rules for Atlas

1. **Route by the student’s decision, not a generic “career” label.** Differentiate exploration, preparation, a current opening, academic credit, pre-health advising, work-study, and immigration authorization.
2. **Store owner links and retrieval dates—not copied job feeds.** A dynamic event or listing needs `source_checked_at` and `dynamic_check_required`.
3. **Keep evidence types separate.** “UNC links this tool,” “a current opportunity is listed,” “the student qualifies,” and “the student should accept it” are four different claims.
4. **Name the office that owns a rule.** Aid and work-study → aid office; work authorization → ISSS; research opening → OUR/owner lab; professional-school path → HPA; job-search preparation → Career Center.
5. **Do not manufacture a pre-med score.** Atlas can help a student inspect duties, supervision, logistics, and stated requirements; it cannot declare an activity meaningful, clinical, application-worthy, or competitive from a title alone.
6. **Never normalize résumé exaggeration.** Application materials must remain truthful; the older UCS policy specifically treats knowingly inaccurate profile/resume information as a serious matter, but current conduct details should be verified through current UNC policies. [UCS student recruiting policy, historical](https://careers.unc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Fall-2022-and-Spring-2023-On-Campus-Recruiting-Policies-and-Job-Offer-Policies-for-Students_Updated_October_4_2022.pdf)

```text
  source_checked_at

```

## Evidence limits

- This packet does not identify a current job, internship, fair, appointment slot, alumni connection, funding award, or employer deadline.
- It does not establish that an employer will respond, a student qualifies, or a role meets any particular pre-health, academic-credit, immigration, or work-study need.
- The Career Center’s linked external tools may change; UNC linking a tool is not a claim that every user can access it or that its results are accurate for a particular person.

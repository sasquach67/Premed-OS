## Routing rule

Campus Recreation owns ordinary recreation access, program registration, and facility questions. It is **not** medical, injury, nutrition, or emergency-care guidance. For an immediate emergency, use 911 / UNC emergency procedures rather than a recreation program.

## Evidence notes Atlas should preserve

- Campus Recreation’s [hours page](https://campusrec.unc.edu/facilities/hours/) explicitly says hours are subject to change and provides live facility status/occupancy routes. Never hardcode a semester timetable as a persistent recommendation.
- [Group Fitness](https://campusrec.unc.edu/program/group-fitness/) says instructors offer modifications and welcomes all levels, but this is an inclusion statement—not individualized exercise or clinical advice.
- The [Outdoor Education Center](https://campusrec.unc.edu/facility-list/oec/) is an official 67-acre Campus Recreation site with open-recreation and programmed spaces. Its trail/court availability, signage, conditions, and reservation rules still control on the day of use.
- [Accessible facilities](https://campusrec.unc.edu/about-us/commitment-to-inclusion/accessible-facilities/) and [accessible equipment](https://campusrec.unc.edu/about-us/commitment-to-inclusion/accessible-equipment/) document features such as ramps/elevators/pool lifts, adaptive equipment and staff assistance. Treat this as a starting route, not a guarantee of suitability for an individual.
- [Expeditions](https://campusrec.unc.edu/programs/carolinaadventures/expeditions/) describes a program emergency action process and weather policies. That is a program-operational claim, not a claim that a trip is risk-free or a substitute for emergency services.

## What this packet deliberately does not do

- Rank gyms, clubs, trainers, trips, or facilities.
- Recommend workouts, rehabilitation, nutrition, weight loss, supplements, or medical clearance decisions.
- Promise membership, an intramural roster, club admission, equipment, trip seats, refunds, accessibility adjustments, or facility opening.
- Copy volatile schedules, prices, or full club lists into Atlas as durable data.

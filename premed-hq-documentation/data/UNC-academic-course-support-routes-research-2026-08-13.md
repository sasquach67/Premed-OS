# UNC academic and course-support routes — official-source research packet

**Status:** primary-source research packet  
**Retrieved:** 2026-08-13  
**Scope:** official UNC–Chapel Hill routes an undergraduate can use when a course, writing project, research assignment, academic-planning question, or approved accommodation creates a concrete need. This is a routing map, not a promise of a grade outcome, available seat, or individual accommodation.

## Executive answer

UNC has a useful but deliberately segmented support stack:

1. **A course-specific concept or study problem** → check the [Learning Center peer-tutoring list](https://learningcenter.unc.edu/appointment-peer-tutoring/) and the [STEM learning-group routes](https://learningcenter.unc.edu/services/stem/); for several Biology courses, use the department's [Bio Peer Mentoring / SI route](https://bio.unc.edu/course-info/tutoring/).
2. **A study-system, time-management, note-taking, or exam-preparation problem in any course** → book [Learning Center Academic Coaching](https://learningcenter.unc.edu/services/academic-coaching/).
3. **A paper, lab report, application essay, presentation, or draft problem** → use the [Writing Center](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/) for coaching or asynchronous draft feedback.
4. **A course choice, major/minor, registration, academic-status, or longer-term academic-path question** → see the assigned advisor through [UNC Advising](https://advising.unc.edu/).
5. **A research question or source-finding/citation problem** → request a [UNC Library research consultation](https://library.unc.edu/schedule-research-consultations/).
6. **A disability, chronic-condition, pregnancy, or religious-accommodation barrier** → begin with the [University Compliance Office Student Accommodations process](https://compliance.unc.edu/access-and-accommodations/student-accommodations/); after approval, use the [Testing Center student process](https://compliance.unc.edu/access-and-accommodations/testing-center/testing-center-students/) where appropriate.
7. **A question about course content, grading rules, or assignment permission** → the instructor, TA, course Canvas site, and published syllabus remain the owning route. University-wide support cannot override an instructor's assignment, assessment, or grade decision.

The student-facing rule is: **match the obstacle to the owner; use a general support service where it fits; verify the current course/section and appointment availability before treating it as usable.**

## Product rules for Atlas / Premed OS

1. **Use need-based routing, not a generic “get help” button.** A student stuck on CHEM 241 concepts, a student choosing next term's schedule, and a student with approved extended-time accommodations need different owners.
2. **Tag every support record with scope:** `strategy_any_course`, `course_listed_only`, `section_dependent`, `writing_process`, `research_process`, `planning`, `approved_accommodation_only`, or `instructor_owned`.
3. **Keep live availability outside the corpus.** Store the official URL and retrieval date; open the live scheduler/course list for a current slot or coverage decision.
4. **Do not make grade, performance, or access guarantees.** “Tutoring available” never means “will raise grade”; “may request” never means “will be approved.”
5. **Preserve academic-integrity boundaries.** Support can explain concepts, give feedback, or help plan work. It cannot be presented as doing an assignment, writing a student's work, or overriding course collaboration rules.
6. **Treat accommodations as sensitive routing data.** No diagnosis, documentation, or accommodation detail should be stored unless the student intentionally supplies it; do not use it for an inference about ability, workload, or career suitability.
7. **Use official source ownership.** Course-specific policy → instructor/TA/syllabus; program/degree rules → catalog/department/Registrar; access barrier → UCO; study strategy → Learning Center.

## What this pass does not establish

- It does not establish individual tutor, coach, advisor, librarian, TA, or instructor availability.
- It does not establish that every BIOL/CHEM section has tutoring, SI, peer mentoring, or identical support.
- It does not establish the correct answer to a course question, a student's academic standing, or the likely effect of any intervention on a grade.
- It does not replace a current syllabus, Canvas announcement, official catalog/Registrar record, or individual accommodation determination.

## Refresh plan

- **At each term start and midterm period:** recheck Learning Center course lists, service hours, and Biology's section-dependent mentoring/SI availability.
- **Before a student acts:** open the live scheduler for appointments, availability, and location.
- **Before a testing need:** recheck UCO/Testing Center procedures and give lead-time warnings without claiming a seat.
- **Annually or after unit reorganization:** validate advising, library, and accommodation URLs and office ownership.

# Orange County / Chapel Hill non-clinical service access: official-source starter network

**Purpose:** a breadth-first discovery layer for the Volunteering experience pillar. This packet identifies organizations with an owner-published volunteer route; it is **not** a claim that a particular shift is open, that a student will be accepted, or that a role is medical/clinical.

**Scope:** Orange County / Chapel Hill–Carrboro first, with two clearly marked nearby/regional routes where the owner expressly serves Orange County. Clinical services, patient care, shadowing, and jobs are intentionally out of scope.

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-13. Owner pages below should be rechecked when a student is ready to act; signup inventories, orientation dates, and capacity change much faster than the organization’s mission.

## How to read this packet

- **Service contact as shown by owner** reports the form of service plainly described by the organization. It does not make an admissions classification or presume what an individual student will do.
- **Live route** means the owner page exposes an application, scheduling portal, contact route, or rolling sign-up. It does **not** mean a specific placement is available today.
- **Constraints** include only conditions the owner publishes. A missing background-check, age, transportation, language, or training rule means **not published on the reviewed page**, not “none.”
- Atlas/Premed OS may store the source, topic, route, constraints, and last-check date. It should link out for signup and must not mirror private forms, participant information, or stale capacity assertions.

## Product rules

1. **Start with the need, not the résumé label.** Example discovery prompts: “food distribution,” “consistent relationship with a family,” “school community,” “older-adult companionship,” “housing construction,” “environmental stewardship.”
2. **Show feasibility before aspirational language.** Surface minimum commitment, background checks, age, recurring time, training, and travel conditions beside the link.
3. **No invented service classification.** The product may repeat an owner’s description of the activity. It should not call an activity “direct service,” “leadership,” “clinical,” or “meaningful” as a fact. If a student logs an experience, they describe their actual duties.
4. **Separate discovery from availability.** A profile can remain in the catalog after a season ends; its current status is `unknown` unless a live first-party sign-up or posting says otherwise.
5. **Preserve recipient privacy.** Never import names, family goals, school records, case notes, background-check data, or contents of application forms into Atlas/Premed OS.
6. **Respect source ownership.** Link to owner pages and record a short factual metadata summary. Do not scrape sign-up inventories, copy protected training content, or represent the product as an authorized placement partner.

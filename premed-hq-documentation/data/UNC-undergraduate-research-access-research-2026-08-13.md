# UNC undergraduate research access — official-source research packet

**Status:** primary-source research packet  
**Retrieved:** 2026-08-13  
**Scope:** how a UNC–Chapel Hill undergraduate can *lawfully and accurately* discover medical/pre-health-relevant research opportunities. This is not a roster of labs that “take undergraduates.”  
**Core rule:** a faculty, lab, center, or department profile establishes a **discovery target**, not an open undergraduate role. Only a current position post or an owner-program application page establishes an open opportunity, and then only through its stated deadline.

## Executive answer

UNC has a maintainable official discovery stack:

1. **Current opening:** the [OUR Opportunities Database](https://our.unc.edu/find/opportunities/) is the university-wide source for current positions and can filter by major, term, and paid / credit / volunteer / work-study status.
2. **Research targeting:** the [School of Medicine Faculty Collaboration Database](https://www.med.unc.edu/oor/faculty-database/), [BBSP Research & Faculty](https://bbsp.unc.edu/research-and-faculty/), departmental faculty/research pages, and [OUR Research Centers and Institutes](https://our.unc.edu/find/research-centers/) identify relevant people and research areas. None promises a vacancy.
3. **Human routing:** [OUR Faculty Liaisons](https://our.unc.edu/liaisons/), OUR staff, student ambassadors, and department Directors of Undergraduate Studies help a student turn an interest into a realistic search.
4. **Structured pathways:** departmental research-for-credit systems, Federal Work-Study / IRIS, and owner-run summer programs provide routes that are separate from a standard paid opening.

The correct Atlas/Premed OS behavior is therefore **“show active official openings + help identify and contact research targets,”** never **“these labs accept undergraduates.”**

## What the current official database proves — and what it does not

On retrieval, the OUR database contained currently dated posts in medical/pre-health-adjacent areas, including clinical research, nursing/biobehavioral work, brain/neuroimaging, perinatal mental health, cancer summer research, and public-health/water research. The listings demonstrated that the database can hold paid, credit, volunteer, and/or work-study routes.

That **does establish** that this is a live official source for time-bounded undergraduate openings. It does **not** establish that any named lab hires continuously, that a similar role will recur, or that an expired listing remains open. Because OUR forbids re-posting without permission, Atlas should take users to the official result rather than reproduce a static copy.

## Departmental pathways that should be modeled separately

These are **not** job listings. They are academic or funding mechanisms that become relevant after a student has a research mentor/project.

- **BIOL 395/395H/495:** Biology says the student finds a lab first; 395 is for UNC biology hypothesis-driven work and does not fit mere technician tasks or clinical-research work. The page describes eligibility, credit-hour workload, approvals, and application timing.
- **BIOL volunteering:** Biology states full-time undergraduates may volunteer up to three hours/week without restriction during the academic year; higher-hour volunteering is time-limited and should transition to pay, work-study, student grant, course credit, or a documented exception. This is an important compliance/context rule, not a claim every lab will provide one of those routes.
- **PSYC/NSCI 395:** psychology/neuroscience majors meeting course/GPA prerequisites may use the credit pathway after finding a willing mentor; it is not a pathway to obtain a mentor.
- **BME research for credit:** a separate BME academic route; link it after project/mentor discovery instead of treating it as an opportunity directory.
- **IRIS / JobX:** work-study pathway for eligible students; must remain a referral to the authenticated system.
- **SURF and summer programs:** funding/program routes with owner-specific eligibility and annual deadlines; separate from a standard in-semester role.

### Product rules

1. **Only `researchOpportunity.status = officially_posted` can render “Open.”** It must cite the exact current post/owner page and have an expiry/recheck date.
2. **`researchTarget` may render “Explore this lab/faculty member,” never “Accepts undergraduates,”** unless a separate current official opening supports the phrase.
3. **The OUR database is link-only by default.** Its explicit no-re-post rule blocks a copied job-board mirror absent written permission.
4. **Keep observed role, compensation mode, term, and eligibility separate.** “Work-study,” “volunteer,” “for credit,” and “paid” are pathways/terms—not interchangeable labels.
5. **Show a source freshness label.** Current opening: recheck at posted end date (or more often). Directory target: recheck at display/use. Program rules/deadlines: recheck each term or application cycle.
6. **Use human routing when fit is unclear.** Direct the student to an OUR liaison/staff member or the major’s Director of Undergraduate Studies rather than inventing an eligibility answer.

## Student-facing route, without overclaiming

1. Pick 2–3 research topics and a practical constraint (hours, term, paid/credit/volunteer/work-study).
2. Search the current [OUR Opportunities Database](https://our.unc.edu/find/opportunities/) first. Save the official link and deadline—not a copied listing.
3. If no match is open, identify 5–10 research targets using BBSP, the School of Medicine database, relevant department faculty pages, and the OUR centers directory.
4. Ask the appropriate [OUR Faculty Liaison](https://our.unc.edu/liaisons/) or OUR staff how to target the search; then contact a potential mentor professionally. An affirmative response from a mentor is the evidence of availability.
5. Once a mentor/project exists, choose the correct mechanism: paid role, work-study/IRIS, volunteer within departmental policy, course credit, or a funding/summer program.
6. Recheck all term-specific credit rules, application forms, and deadlines before acting.

## Evidence limits and next refresh

- This pass intentionally does **not** build a one-time list of “UNC labs that take undergraduates.” The official sources do not justify that claim.
- It also does not copy individual OUR listings, because the database prohibits reposting without written permission.
- Revisit the Opportunities Database on a regular cadence during a search; verify target profiles at the moment of outreach; and recheck all academic/funding program requirements each term/cycle.
- The next lawful expansion is a **link-based target catalog** (faculty/lab/center profiles, topic tags, and citations) plus an **official-current-opening pointer**, never a scraped or static job mirror.

# S-o1 — AMCAS, AACOMAS, and TMDSAS observation categorization

**Status:** primary-source research packet  
**Retrieved:** August 13, 2026  
**Scope:** application-system category wording only. This packet does not decide how an admissions office will value any activity.

## Decision-critical answer

| Application service | What its official material says | Can it be used to assert that PA/NP/other non-physician observation belongs in a physician-shadowing category? |
|---|---|---|
| **AMCAS** | AMCAS exposes an experience type labeled **“Physician Shadowing/Clinical Observation.”** Its Applicant Guide tells the applicant to select the type that “best represents” the experience and says selection is self-identified. The guide and Work/Activities FAQ reviewed here provide **no category-specific definition** of that label and do **not** mention physician assistants, nurse practitioners, or other non-physician clinicians in relation to it. | **No. AAMC does not state this.** The label includes “Physician,” but the reviewed primary sources do not define its clinician scope. Premed OS must not infer that PA/NP observation is included or excluded. |
| **AACOMAS** | AACOMAS has no separate published “shadowing” type in its Experience Types list. It tells applicants to select the category that best fits the responsibilities, and says programs may differ. Its closest official category is **Healthcare Experience**. | **No explicit answer.** AACOMAS’s published definition does not mention shadowing, physicians, PAs, or NPs. | **TMDSAS** | TMDSAS uses **Healthcare Activities** and explicitly gives “shadowing” as an example. Its wording names formal direct observation/participation “with a physician or podiatrist.” | **TMDSAS distinguishes this more narrowly in its example wording.** It does not explicitly say whether observing a PA/NP belongs there; do not extend “physician or podiatrist” to other clinicians by inference. |

## AMCAS: exact official wording and the non-answer

The **2027 AMCAS Applicant Guide** says:

> “From the drop-down list, select the experience type that best represents the experience you wish to include. Given that the information is self-identified, you must decide which option fits best for each experience.”

AMCAS’s Work/Activities FAQ does not provide a category definition for **Physician Shadowing/Clinical Observation**. The guide/application worksheet publishes the exact option label, but no separate definition of what counts as a physician, clinical observation, or non-physician observation.

## AACOMAS: exact official category definitions

AACOMAS says:

> “Note that since each program's definitions and requirements vary, AACOMAS has no specific preference as to which category you choose.”

> “Your experience's individual job description and responsibilities should determine the category you select.”

| AACOMAS experience type | Exact official definition |
|---|---|
| Extracurricular Activities | “Related activities you would like your selected programs to review; for example, academic clubs and competitive teams. Do not include paid work experience in this section.” |
| Healthcare Experience | “Both paid and unpaid work in a health or health-related field where you are not directly responsible for a patient's care, but may still have patient interaction; for example, filling prescriptions, performing clerical work, delivering patient food, cleaning patients and/or their rooms, administering food or medication, taking vitals or other record-keeping information, working as a scribe, CNA (depending on job description), medical assistant, etc.” |
| Non-Healthcare Employment | “Paid work done outside of the health care field or a research lab; for example, a retail or restaurant job.” |
| Research | “Any experience working on a research project, preferably in addition to or outside of regular classroom work. This may include student research positions, research technician positions, summer research student, master’s rotational student, etc.” |
| Teaching Experience | “Experiences in which you were in charge of instructing others, such as a teaching assistant, tutor, etc.” |
| Non-Healthcare Volunteer or Community Enrichment | “Volunteer work done outside of the health care field; for example, working for Habitat for Humanity, tutoring students, participating in or working for a fundraiser walk or blood drive, etc.” |

**Observation finding:** AACOMAS’s reviewed official list has no separate “Physician Shadowing/Clinical Observation” category and never expressly identifies observation of a physician, PA, NP, or other clinician. The service’s own text tells applicants to choose based on duties and warns that individual programs can differ. This is not a basis to label non-physician observation automatically.

## TMDSAS: exact official wording

TMDSAS does not use a separate physician-shadowing bucket. Under **Healthcare Activities**, it says:

> “List all direct healthcare-related activities you have participated in since graduating high school. For example, these may include: shadowing, scribing, clinical research, serving or working as a patient care tech, a nurse, and any direct observation or participation in patient care in a clinic, hospital, or with a physician or podiatrist in a formal way.”

TMDSAS also says:

> “TMDSAS does not provide advising to applicants on activity category selection.”

**Observation finding:** this expressly includes shadowing in Healthcare Activities and names formal direct observation/participation with “a physician or podiatrist.” It does **not** say whether observation of a PA, NP, or other clinician should be classified there. No extension should be made without an official clarification.

## Product-safe ruling

1. **Do not automatically map PA/NP/non-physician observation to AMCAS’s `Physician Shadowing/Clinical Observation`.** AAMC does not state that it belongs there.
2. Store the **clinician role observed** separately from the student’s self-chosen application category. That preserves the factual record without inventing a classification rule.
3. When a student asks where to classify non-physician observation, Atlas/Premed OS should say: **“The reviewed official instructions do not explicitly answer this. Confirm with the target application service or your advising office before submitting.”**
4. TMDSAS and AACOMAS wording should not be used to fill AMCAS’s gap; they are separate application systems with separate instructions.

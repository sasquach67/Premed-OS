# UNC study abroad and global opportunities: safe routing rules

**Research date:** 2026-08-14 (America/New_York)  
**Scope:** First-party UNC–Chapel Hill sources. This packet maps decisions to owners; it is not a program catalog, a credit decision, travel clearance, financial-aid determination, visa ruling, or safety assessment.

## Decision in one screen

UNC's [Study Abroad Office](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/) is the primary campus unit for undergraduate domestic and international semester, summer, and break programs, particularly for College students (including Honors Carolina) and some professional schools. The live [Program Search](https://heelsabroad.unc.edu/) is the discovery surface; a program page is not an assurance that the program is accepting applicants, affordable, accessible, suitable for a pre-health timeline, or available to a particular student. [UNC Undergraduate Catalog: Study Abroad](https://unc-public.courseleaf.com/undergraduate/academic-enrichment/study-abroad/)

| Student question | Owner route | First action | Never infer |
|---|---|---|---|
| “What programs exist for my term/location?” | [UNC Program Search](https://heelsabroad.unc.edu/) | Search live; then read the owner page’s dates, eligibility, cost, application, and sponsor. | That search inclusion means capacity, admission, or a good academic fit. |
| “Will this keep me on track academically?” | Study Abroad Office/sponsoring school **plus** academic advisor/department. | Ask about the actual program and planned courses before committing. | That a course title, past equivalency, or a major label guarantees a requirement. |
| “Does this count for UNC credit?” | Study Abroad Office or sponsoring UNC school. | Follow the program’s approval process and preserve the official documents requested. | That credit posts before completion or that a passing local grade is automatically enough. |
| “Can financial aid or a scholarship pay for it?” | [Office of Scholarships and Student Aid](https://studentaid.unc.edu/) + Study Abroad Office. | Compare the program budget, student aid record, aid conditions, and current scholarship rules. | That aid applies equally to every program or covers every travel expense. |
| “Can I do research, service, an internship, or a virtual program abroad?” | Study Abroad Office / sponsoring unit. | Establish whether it is an **approved** academic program and what credit/approval route applies. | That an outside opportunity automatically earns UNC credit or Experiential Education credit. |
| “I have accessibility, health, passport, visa, or safety concerns.” | Study Abroad Office first; then the appropriate owner (accommodations, clinician, insurer, consulate, ISSS, or program). | Raise the concern early and follow current official guidance. | That a general program page can assess individual health, visa, accommodation, or travel risk. |

## What UNC officially establishes

### Program types and who owns them

The catalog describes approved credit-bearing options across semesters, summer, academic year, and short-term formats, including exchanges, faculty-led programs, direct enrollment, internships, research, service-learning, domestic programs, and virtual programs. Programs vary in cost, length, location, program type, and academic format. Student-initiated academic-credit programs require a petition for approval through the Study Abroad Office rather than an assumption that outside enrollment will transfer. [UNC Undergraduate Catalog: Study Abroad](https://unc-public.courseleaf.com/undergraduate/academic-enrichment/study-abroad/)

**Atlas routing rule:** separate four fields that are often collapsed:

`program discovered` ≠ `program approved` ≠ `student admitted` ≠ `credit/aid posted`.

### Credit follows the approved process and completed record

UNC says credit approval is coordinated by the Study Abroad Office or the UNC school sponsoring the program, and that equivalent credit is applied after the program ends and the office receives an official transcript/grade report. The catalog states that a C or equivalent is generally needed for credit; C- or below does not earn credit or satisfy academic requirements and can affect the UNC GPA as a failed course. Conversion can apply when units are not semester hours. [UNC Undergraduate Catalog: Study Abroad](https://unc-public.courseleaf.com/undergraduate/academic-enrichment/study-abroad/)

This means a student should not schedule graduation, prerequisites, professional-school preparation, or a later course sequence solely on a hoped-for equivalency. They need the current owner’s approval path and enough calendar slack for official records after the program.

### Financial aid is possible, individual, and program-dependent

The catalog says financial aid can be used toward study-abroad costs and that UNC awards study-abroad scholarships, but neither statement establishes a particular student's eligibility, package, disbursement timing, remaining costs, or cancellation liability. The correct route is a current aid review plus the program’s published budget and requirements. [UNC Undergraduate Catalog: Study Abroad](https://unc-public.courseleaf.com/undergraduate/academic-enrichment/study-abroad/) · [UNC Scholarships and Student Aid](https://studentaid.unc.edu/)

### High-Impact / Experiential Education needs precise language

UNC’s catalog says students who participate in approved study-abroad programs earn Experiential Education/High-Impact Experience General Education credit, including virtual programs. Atlas may repeat that only where the student is using an approved program and should still route unusual cases to the sponsoring office. Do not turn that statement into “any travel, volunteering, remote internship, or international experience counts.” [UNC Undergraduate Catalog: Study Abroad](https://unc-public.courseleaf.com/undergraduate/academic-enrichment/study-abroad/)

## Scenario playbook

| Situation | Action sequence | Verification checklist |
|---|---|---|
| Exploring without a destination yet | Attend/locate current Study Abroad advising or information session → use live Program Search → build a short comparison list. | Term, program type, location, sponsor, eligibility, costs, deadlines, and academic fit. |
| Pre-health student protecting prerequisite sequencing | Share the actual four-year/course plan with academic advising and Study Abroad before applying. | Course approvals/equivalencies, lab availability, MCAT/application timing, transcript timing, and fallback schedule. |
| Research/service/internship abroad | Identify the exact activity and sponsoring organization → ask whether the route is approved for academic credit → verify supervision, hours, risk/process, and post-program documentation. | Approval status, credit owner, insurance/field requirements, terms, costs, and whether the activity is academic rather than merely travel. |
| Concerned about cost | Build a line-item budget from the current program page → meet with aid owner → check current study-abroad scholarships before their own deadlines. | Tuition/program fee, housing, flights, visa/passport, local transportation, deposits, insurance, aid eligibility/disbursement, and cancellation terms. |
| Needs disability/pregnancy/religious accommodation | Contact the accommodation owner early and coordinate with Study Abroad/program as appropriate. | Individual documentation/process, host-country/program limits, timing, and privacy handling. |
| Is an international student at UNC | Consult ISSS before relying on travel/re-entry assumptions. | Immigration document, visa/re-entry, enrollment, and travel specifics for that individual. |
| Wants a program during a specific break | Read only the live term/program page; application dates change. | Deadline, nomination/application sequence, passport/visa lead time, program deposit, and transcript calendar. |

## Product rules for Atlas

1. **Use live discovery, not a copied program catalog.** Persist owner URL plus `source_checked_at`; program availability, deadlines, cost, and capacity change.
2. **Ask which decision the student is actually making.** Discovery, academic fit, approval, admission, aid, accommodation, travel documents, and credit posting have different owners.
3. **Show a “verify before committing money” panel.** At minimum: official program page, credit/academic plan, aid review, deposit/cancellation terms, passport/visa timing, and any individual accessibility/health/re-entry question.
4. **Never promise travel safety or medical suitability.** Atlas can route to current University/program resources but must not substitute for clinician, insurer, government, or emergency guidance.
5. **Label history correctly.** A prior program, former deadline, older student anecdote, or old course-equivalency is a research lead—not a current offer or guarantee.

## Current workflow clarifications

- Begin program exploration with [Study Abroad advising and events](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/advising-events/), then use [Find a Program](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/find-program/). Each has live details; no fixed deadline should be copied into Atlas without a current check.
- [UNC's credit workflow](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/earning-credit-from-spring-2021-forward/) says the pre-approved-course database is neither exhaustive nor a guarantee: hosts may not offer a course in the student’s term and approval entries expire. Accepted students use the Credit Portal; other course questions may need the departmental reviewer or `credit_abroad@unc.edu`.
- The student is responsible for getting an official transcript to the Study Abroad Office. UNC’s catalog warns that a missing transcript after the last day of the next fall/spring term can yield an `SI` and affect academic standing, satisfactory academic progress, or graduation. Treat that as a planning safeguard, not a prediction.
- The live funding sequence is [Funding 101](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/funding-101/), [Study Abroad Office scholarships](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/study-abroad-office-scholarships/), the [scholarship database](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/scholarship-database/), and [other funding sources](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/other-funding-sources/). Each contains dynamic eligibility/deadline information.
- Use [health and safety](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/health-safety/) and [travel-approval procedures](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/travel-approval-procedures/) as current owner routes; neither replaces individual clinical, insurance, government, or emergency advice.

## Source register and refresh rules

1. [UNC Study Abroad Office](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/) — main advising/program owner. Recheck live for appointments, events, application instructions, deadlines, and policies.
2. [UNC Program Search](https://heelsabroad.unc.edu/) — live program-discovery surface. Listings are time-sensitive and may have access/eligibility conditions.
3. [UNC Undergraduate Catalog: Study Abroad](https://catalog.unc.edu/undergraduate/academic-enrichment/study-abroad/) — official academic-policy baseline. Recheck the catalog year and specific sponsoring school/program for case-level decisions.
4. [UNC Scholarships and Student Aid](https://studentaid.unc.edu/) — individual financial-aid owner. Recheck per aid year and against the student’s actual record.
5. [UNC International Student and Scholar Services](https://isss.unc.edu/) — individualized immigration/travel-work authorization owner.
6. [UNC student accommodations](https://compliance.unc.edu/) — individualized accommodation route; do not infer an outcome or speed.
7. [UNC Study Abroad credit route](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/earning-credit-from-spring-2021-forward/) — current equivalency/approval workflow and database limitations.
8. [UNC Study Abroad health and safety](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/health-safety/) · [travel-approval procedures](https://studyabroad.unc.edu/travel-approval-procedures/) — current process routes; verify case-specific requirements live.

## Evidence limits

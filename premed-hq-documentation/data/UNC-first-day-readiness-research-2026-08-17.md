# UNC first-day readiness: official routes and Fall 2026 facts

**Research date:** 2026-08-17 (America/New_York)  
**Scope:** A narrowly practical first-day packet for a UNC–Chapel Hill undergraduate. It separates verified Fall 2026 facts from student-specific checks that must be completed in authenticated systems. It is not an individual schedule, enrollment decision, or guaranteed travel plan.

## Verified anchors

- **Monday, August 17, 2026:** Main Campus Session A classes and late registration begin. [UNC Registrar: Fall 2026 calendar](https://registrar.unc.edu/registrar-calendar-fall/)
- **Friday, August 21, 11:59 p.m.:** last day to add Fall classes in ConnectCarolina; after that, students must visit the academic department to add a course. [Registrar calendar](https://registrar.unc.edu/registrar-calendar-fall/)
- **Friday, August 28, 5 p.m.:** last day for undergraduates to drop a class in ConnectCarolina without a W; full-time students must maintain 12 credit hours. This is a decision point, not a recommendation to drop. [Registrar calendar](https://registrar.unc.edu/registrar-calendar-fall/)
- All UNC–Chapel Hill faculty, students, and staff with an active **Onyen** have Canvas access. Official course rosters are automatically added from ConnectCarolina enrollment data. [UNC Educational Technologies: Canvas](https://edtech.unc.edu/service/canvas/)

## The night-before sequence

1. **Confirm the authoritative schedule.** Sign into [ConnectCarolina](https://connectcarolina.unc.edu/) and record each enrolled section's course, component (lecture/lab/recitation), start/end time, building/room, and any same-day change. The authenticated schedule—not a catalog listing or saved screenshot—controls enrollment and section details. [ConnectCarolina](https://connectcarolina.unc.edu/) · [Registrar academic-calendar hub](https://registrar.unc.edu/academic-calendar/)
2. **Open Canvas at [canvas.unc.edu](https://canvas.unc.edu/) with Onyen.** Check every *published* course for a welcome item, syllabus/Course Resources, first meeting instructions, materials, and announcements. Canvas is UNC's central hub for course materials, assignments, grades, and instructor/student communication; a missing course may reflect publication timing, not an enrollment conclusion. [UNC Canvas service](https://edtech.unc.edu/service/canvas/)
   - In Canvas, set notification preferences and check the Dashboard/To Do list and Calendar for aggregated current-course activity. The Canvas Help menu provides 24/7 chat and phone support; use [UNC Help](https://help.unc.edu/) for UNC-specific/roster questions. [UNC Canvas FAQ](https://edtech.unc.edu/service/canvas/canvas-faq/) · [UNC Canvas service](https://edtech.unc.edu/service/canvas/)
3. **Make one simple route card for each class.** For each room, save the official [Campus Locations directory](https://maps.unc.edu/campus-locations/) entry or the [main UNC map](https://maps.unc.edu/); write the building's exact name/abbreviation as it appears in the schedule. Do not depend on a scraped or guessed building pin. The directory is an official, live reference for commonly used campus locations. [Campus Locations](https://maps.unc.edu/campus-locations/)
4. **Choose a transport plan plus a fallback.** Check the current route map, schedule, and service alerts directly with [Chapel Hill Transit](https://www.townofchapelhill.org/government/departments-services/transit) for local bus service. For campus traffic, parking, campus transit, and current alerts, use [Move UNC](https://move.unc.edu/). These are separate owners; routes, detours, stops, permits, and availability are dynamic. [Move UNC](https://move.unc.edu/) · [Chapel Hill Transit](https://www.townofchapelhill.org/government/departments-services/transit)
5. **Pack only the opening-day essentials:** charged phone/laptop, charger, ID, water, notebook, and any material explicitly required by the course. Put the first building/room and instructor/TA contact route on the phone lock screen or a paper note.
6. **Stop early enough to sleep.** No optional academic catch-up, lab outreach, or long-term pre-med planning needs to happen tonight; the concrete win is arriving with the correct live schedule, a working Canvas login, and a tested route.

## Morning-of sequence

1. Reopen ConnectCarolina and Canvas for any overnight room, message, or publication changes.
2. Recheck live transit/parking alerts immediately before leaving. Move UNC notes Fall 2026 move-in was Aug. 12–16 and urged people on campus to plan for traffic; that is a dated disruption notice, not evidence of today's conditions. [Move UNC Fall 2026 notice](https://move.unc.edu/)
3. Leave with a meaningful buffer, then use the official map/directory to confirm the building entrance and room. A map result does not establish a safe/open walking route or a bus arrival time.
4. Get to the building early enough to find the room, settle, and note the instructor's first instructions. If the listed room is closed/changed, use the course's Canvas/instructor communication route first; do not infer a new location from another section.

## In each first class

- Verify that the meeting, room, section, and course component match the enrolled schedule.
- Attend unless the course itself communicates otherwise. UNC's attendance policy makes regular attendance a student obligation and says each course's attendance policy is communicated through its syllabus on the first day. [UNC attendance, grading, and examination policy](https://catalog.unc.edu/policies-procedures/attendance-grading-examination/)
- Capture only the actionable items: required materials/access code, first assignment, office hours/contact channel, attendance expectations, and the next deadline. The instructor, syllabus, and course Canvas site own course-specific content, grading, assessment, and assignment-permission rules. [UNC academic-support routing](UNC-academic-course-support-routes-research-2026-08-13.md)
- If something conflicts across Canvas, the syllabus, and spoken directions, preserve the conflict and ask the instructor/TA through the published course route. Do not silently choose one source.

## After the final class

1. Update the route cards with anything learned about entrances, realistic transitions, and supplies.
2. Re-read each Canvas course and put the first actual deadlines into the student's preferred planner.
3. If an enrolled course, component, hold, waitlist, or restriction looks wrong, use the *live section detail* in ConnectCarolina and then the explicitly named department/advisor owner. Do not predict a waitlist outcome or assume a catalog course is available. [UNC registration and course access packet](UNC-registration-and-course-access-routes-research-2026-08-14.md)
4. For academic-strategy support (rather than an immediate course-specific question), UNC's Learning Center offers academic coaching, while instructor/TA/course materials remain the owner for the class itself. [UNC academic-support routing](UNC-academic-course-support-routes-research-2026-08-13.md)

### Evidence limits

- This packet does **not** know the student's actual class locations, Canvas publication state, holds, enrollment status, route, bus arrival, parking eligibility, or accessibility needs.
- A Canvas course's absence is not proof of a registration problem; its publication status and roster timing may differ. Check ConnectCarolina, then use the course/department's official contact route.
- Transit/parking and map pages are wayfinding sources, not guarantees of arrival, access, safety, or availability.

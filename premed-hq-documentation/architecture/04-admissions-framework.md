# Admissions-Aware Framework

## Purpose

This file defines how Premed OS should reason about pre-med data without overgeneralizing metrics or claiming to predict admissions outcomes.

## Core rule

Use the metrics and structures that matter for the domain being tracked.

## Examples

### Clinical

Relevant:

- Hours
- Duration
- Continuity
- Patient contact
- Setting
- Responsibility
- Verification
- Recency

### Volunteering

Relevant:

- Hours
- Continuity
- Population or need
- Direct versus indirect service
- Responsibility
- Recurrence
- Reach

### Shadowing

Relevant:

- Hours
- Specialty
- Physician
- Setting
- Dates
- Breadth
- Continuity

### Research

Relevant:

- Project stage
- Role
- Independence
- Methods
- Outputs
- Mentor
- Presentation or publication status
- Duration

### Extracurriculars

Do not center hours.

Relevant:

- Role
- Leadership level
- Responsibility
- Initiatives
- Team size
- Reach
- Outcomes
- Advancement
- Organizational impact
- Sustainability

### Academics

Relevant:

- GPA
- Science GPA
- Credits
- Prerequisites
- Degree requirements
- Academic trend
- Course sequencing
- Repeats or withdrawals
- Transfer and AP credit

### MCAT

Relevant:

- Test date
- Study phase
- Practice scores
- Section performance
- Accuracy
- Timing
- Weak topics
- Readiness

### Letters

Relevant:

- Recommender category
- Relationship
- Eligibility
- Request date
- Deadline
- Status
- Supporting materials
- School assignment

## Guardrails

- Do not produce a universal readiness score without transparent components.
- Do not equate raw quantity with quality.
- Do not imply certainty about admission.
- Preserve factual, exportable records.

## Purpose

Capture the grounded, real-world research about pre-med pathways that the platform's admissions reasoning should reflect — what pre-meds and admissions actually report, so the product's defaults and guidance match reality rather than assumption.

This is the evidence layer feeding `04a` (the model) and `04c` (the intelligence).

## Planned sections

- Pathway variants (traditional 4-year, gap year(s), post-bacc, career changer, BS/MD)
- Typical experience-hour ranges by category, with sourcing and the caveat that ranges are not targets
- Prerequisite and coursework sequencing patterns
- MCAT timing patterns relative to application cycle
- Application timeline (primary open, secondary cadence, interview season, decisions)
- Source hierarchy for this research (per `architecture/02` retrieval: official > peer-reviewed > reputable community)
- Explicit uncertainty and regional/school variation notes

## Do not

Do not present ranges as goals or imply admissions certainty. Preserve source traceability.

# Product Vision

## Product identity

Premed OS is an intelligent operating system for pre-med students and applicants. It should unify planning, tracking, documentation, analysis, and application preparation without becoming a generic spreadsheet, Notion template, or life-coaching product.

## Primary outcomes

The product should help users:

- Maintain accurate records
- Reduce duplicate data entry
- Understand what needs attention
- Plan academic and extracurricular progress
- Prepare application-ready records
- Connect related information across the entire workspace
- Operate efficiently from one central system

## Product principles

- Capture once, reuse everywhere
- Explain every recommendation
- Favor useful workflows over decorative dashboards
- Preserve domain-specific logic
- Use admissions-aware structures without inventing admissions guarantees
- Remain usable for new students and efficient for power users
- Protect privacy and avoid collection of unnecessary sensitive information

## Target users

- High-school students planning a pre-med path
- College pre-med students
- Gap-year applicants
- Current application-cycle users
- Future advisor and institutional users

## Non-goals

Premed OS should not:

- Psychologically profile users
- Pretend to predict admissions outcomes with certainty
- Turn every tab into an hours tracker
- Force all domains into the same metrics
- Generate unnecessary content merely to appear intelligent

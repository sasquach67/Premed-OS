Why nothing is ever pre-filled
If HQ shipped a number in that box, it would become the bar in your head no matter what the caption underneath said, and you would spend two years measuring yourself against something the app made up. There is no verified figure for expected clinical hours, so the only honest starting point is your own rate and your own runway.

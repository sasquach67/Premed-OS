What this is doing

The hero is one recommendation, not a catalog. HQ already knows your phase, your weakest categories, and what you own — so it answers instead of listing. The shelf below is for browsing, not deciding.

It reads what's scheduled and links out. The recommendation is derived from the block you're already scheduled to do, and Open takes you to the resource. No role labels, no durations, no inserting things into your session — the student knows whether they're learning it or brushing up. If nothing scheduled maps to a resource, the hero says so in one line rather than inventing a suggestion.

Expiry appears only on paid items. Free resources carry no expiry field at all — Khan Academy and Jack Westin just show Free. Only things you paid for enter the subscription tracker, and a warning fires only when the expiry actually collides with a planned block, not on a countdown.

Grouped by purpose, not by price. Content review · Question banks · Decks · Full-lengths — matching the plan's phases, so the group you need is the group that's lit.

No prices except on things you already bought, no affiliate links, no "upgrade" anywhere. Free options lead where they're competitive. The gap notice points at Pankow because it's free and unstarted — not at something to buy.

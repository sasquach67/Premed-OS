# Feature-state coverage sweep

This review-only sheet gives otherwise under-shown specification states a visible place in the mockup lab. It is **not** a new product tab or a substitute for the real product mockups.

- `?area=academics` covers the lower-frequency academic learning, grade, and lifecycle states.
- `?area=mcat` covers the missing session, plan, shelf, M2M, and statistics states.
- `?area=experiences` covers the shared experience reads, reflection mechanism, Discover boundary, and honest no-data treatments.
- `?area=overview` covers cross-pillar decision states and the confirmed quarterly-goal model.

The feature’s chip identifies its real home surface. Core workflow mockups continue to own the actual page architecture; this sheet ensures a reviewer can see every extra state before implementation.

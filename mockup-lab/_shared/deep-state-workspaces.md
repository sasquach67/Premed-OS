# Deep state workspaces

Companion notes for `deep-state-workspaces.html`.

These are not new product tabs. They are full mockups of states that previously
appeared only as small cards in the coverage sweep. Each state remains attached
to its canonical owner:

- Academics: learning signals in Class Center, grade decisions in Grades, and
  the end-of-term rollover ritual.
- MCAT: the working session, phase-gated Test Day panel, and full-length
  validity context. Test Day remains a Dashboard panel, never an eighth tab.
- Overview: one Quarterly Goals interaction with its typed-goal default,
  confirmation-first editor, and no-goal/no-bar state. In the lab those three
  states use the bottom A/B/C selector; they are not product tabs.

The surfaces deliberately distinguish measured facts, student-confirmed
interpretation, and insufficient evidence. They do not invent scores, pace,
progress, or data.

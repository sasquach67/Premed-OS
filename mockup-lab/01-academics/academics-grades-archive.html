How these numbers are produced
rules are data, not logic — re-verify in your application year
AMCAS truncates rather than rounds. 3.667 reports as 3.66.
Every attempt of a repeated course counts. There is no AMCAS grade replacement, unlike UNC's policy.
All postsecondary institutions are included, not just UNC.
Classification follows course content, not the title or the department — the live question for a neuroscience major, where some courses classify as Biology and some as Other.
AMCAS mechanics change between cycles and this is a snapshot. Every rule above is configurable data with a cited guide version, so a rule change is a data edit rather than a rebuild.

# Academics · Learning signals — decisions

**Status:** PROPOSED · Stage-A coverage

## Behaviour

- This is a small, pull-first panel inside a **STEM class’s Overview**, beneath
  that class’s next action. It is not a Daily tab, alert feed, or generic study
  coach.
- It shows at most three entries, always **cause → consequence → one action**.
  The action opens the existing owner: Topics/review, Materials, Assignments,
  or the recall summary.
- A signal is absent until its required class evidence exists. Writing and
  General never render an imitation of this panel.
- A cross-class overlap is only a proposed `TopicLink`; it shows its evidence,
  asks the student to confirm, never merges courses, and never shares credit
  for review automatically.

## Appearance

- The class banner and underline hierarchy stay identical to Class Hub. The
  signal surface belongs inside Overview rather than looking like a sixth tab.
- **A · priority rail** is a quiet bento companion below the next action: short
  evidence rows at left and a narrow “why this appeared” rail at right. It is
  the most operational treatment.
- **B · editorial section** makes the same signals a reading sequence under
  the next action. Cause and consequence lead; the action is a small blue text
  route rather than a second primary button.
- **C · evidence drawer** keeps the Overview work surface almost unchanged and
  opens a slim right-side evidence drawer. It is the most progressive-disclosure
  treatment and makes the cross-class confirmation visibly separate.
- All three use solid-with-depth panels, small square evidence marks, and the
  Academics blue only for routes and evidence. No metric card, gauge, or
  progress bar stands in for a signal.

## Variant question

The product rule is settled; placement composition is not. A/B/C compares a
rail, an editorial section, and a contextual drawer without changing the data
rules. Andy chooses one before any implementation is briefed.

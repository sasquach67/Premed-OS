# Planning Requirements Library — decisions

**File:** `academics-planning-library.html` · **Status:** **SUPERSEDED**
(historical source, 2026-08-26). Its requirement-dossier behavior now lives in
`academics-planner-prototype.html?view=requirements`; this file is unregistered
and is not an approval target.

This was the intermediate replacement for the old numerical “gap and pace”
premise. The integrated Planner now makes the reviewable decision visible:
selected catalog provenance first, candidate-course evidence second, and the
official-audit boundary at every requirement state.

- **Program/track is explicit.** No generic public-health or track inference.
- **Candidate states are not fulfillment.** The only labels are Course
  recorded, Not scheduled, and Manual review; no percent, completed count,
  remaining count, or degree verdict appears.
- **Choice/exclusion/no-double-count logic stays adjacent** to its affected
  node. The mockup demonstrates the neuroscience methods exception.
- **Cohort provenance is captured, not inferred.** Exact matriculation and
  IDEAs catalog year are visible; B.S.P.H. adds Gillings admission term/status.
- **ConnectCarolina remains a first-class action** rather than a footnote.

The full implementation brief remains a reference, not build authority, until
this mockup batch is approved.

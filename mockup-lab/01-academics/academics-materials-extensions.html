Why can a bulky base favor a different alkene than a small base?
A bulky base reaches the less crowded β-hydrogen more easily, so elimination can favor the less substituted alkene.
Ex: Picture a large backpack trying to reach two seats on a bus. It takes the open aisle seat, even if another seat would be more desirable once occupied.

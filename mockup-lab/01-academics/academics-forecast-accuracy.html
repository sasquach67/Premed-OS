What the app claimed, and what happened

When Premed OS says a topic is solid, it usually is.

Every per-review prediction is scored against the attempt that followed it. Nothing here is a guess about the future — it is a record of the past.

"Solid" You recalled it on 8 of the next 9 attempts. Holding up
"Fading" You recalled it on 5 of the next 11 attempts. Holding up
"Likely gone" You still recalled it on 6 of 9 attempts — this call has been pessimistic. Runs pessimistic
Read this as a claim about the app, not about you. A call that misses often means the scheduling is wrong, not that you are behind. Where a band runs consistently off, Premed OS says so rather than quietly continuing to make it.

Roughly forty resolved predictions is the point at which a hit rate stops being noise. Until then Premed OS shows nothing here — and, more importantly, suppresses the forecasts themselves rather than showing them with a caveat.

Below the gate, forecasts do not ship at all. A caveat under an unreliable number is still an unreliable number, and students read the number.

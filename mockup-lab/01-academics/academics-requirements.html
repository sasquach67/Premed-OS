Why this is captured now
capture now, or reconstruct badly in three years
Every attempt of a repeated course counts toward AMCAS. There is no AMCAS grade replacement, unlike UNC's policy — so a retake adds an attempt rather than replacing one.
AMCAS truncates rather than rounds. 3.667 reports as 3.66, not 3.67.
Classification follows course content, not the title or the department — which is the live question for a neuroscience major. Each course is classified when you take it, with the syllabus and your reasoning stored alongside it.
AMCAS mechanics change between cycles. These rules are data, not logic — verify against the official Course Classification Guide in your application year.
